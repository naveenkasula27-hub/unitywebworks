Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

UnityWebWorks ("we", "us", "our") builds websites, apps, ad systems and workflow automation for small businesses. This policy explains how we handle data when you use our website, our live product demos, or connect a Google account to one of our automation workflows.

Google account access

Some of our automation projects use Google APIs (such as Gmail) to send automated transactional notifications — for example, a booking confirmation email to a business owner. When you authorize this access via Google Sign-In:

  • We request only the permission needed to send email on behalf of the connected account, through the Gmail API.
  • We do not read your inbox, your contacts, or any existing email content.
  • We do not sell, share, or transfer your Google account data to any third party.
  • Access is used solely to operate the specific automation you configured (e.g. sending a booking notification).

Data we collect on this site

Our website may collect basic contact details you submit voluntarily through a form (name, email, project details) to respond to your enquiry. We do not sell this information.

Demo products

Our live product demos (such as booking or ordering demos) store the information you enter — name, phone number, appointment or order details — only to operate the demo and send you a confirmation message. These are demo environments, not live commercial transactions, unless stated otherwise on the specific product page.

Data retention & your controls

You can revoke any Google account access granted to us at any time from myaccount.google.com/permissions. To request deletion of any data we hold about you, contact us using the details below.
