Questions

UI/UX Design - answered

Do I need UI/UX design as a separate service, or is it included?

Design is included in every website and app project. This is for when you want design work on its own - a redesign of an existing product, or mockups for a pitch.

How many revisions are included?

One full round is included in the quoted price. Further rounds are scoped and priced separately so costs stay predictable for both of us.

Can you redesign my existing site without touching the code?

Yes. We can deliver Figma mockups of a redesign that your existing team implements, or we implement it ourselves as a separate build.

What does a design handoff include?

A clickable Figma prototype, organized components, and the measurements and assets a developer needs to build it accurately.
